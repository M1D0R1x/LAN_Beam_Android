# LAN Beam privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

LAN Beam moves files between your phone and other devices on the same local network (your Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot).

**What we collect:** nothing. LAN Beam has no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no servers. The app never sends data to the internet.

**Files:** files you choose to share are copied into LAN Beam's private storage on your phone. Files other devices send you are saved in `Download/LANBeam` on your phone. They are only transferred to devices on your local network that open LAN Beam's address while sharing is switched on, and they never leave your network through LAN Beam.

**Who can connect:** any device on the same local network that knows the address shown in the app, while sharing is on. Turn sharing off (switch at the top of the app, or Stop in the notification) when you are done. Avoid sharing sensitive files on public or shared Wi-Fi.

**Permissions:**
- Notifications: shows transfer progress and a Stop button.
- Network and Wi-Fi state: finds the address other devices use to reach this phone.
- Foreground service and wake lock: keeps sharing running at full speed while files are moving.
- Storage (Android 10 and older only): saves received files to Download.

**Settings:** your device name and app preferences are stored on the phone and may be included in Android's own device backup. Shared and received files are excluded from backup.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/M1D0R1x/LAN_Beam_Android/issues
